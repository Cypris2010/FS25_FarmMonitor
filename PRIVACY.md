# Privacy Policy

FarmMonitor does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data on
external servers. It has no telemetry, no analytics, and no user accounts.

## How it works

FarmMonitor runs entirely on your own machine. The mod writes game data to
local JSON files, and the local server displays them in your browser
(localhost / your LAN). No data leaves your computer through the application.

## Diagnostics (optional, local only)

You can optionally enable diagnostic features:

- **Performance logging** writes timing lines to a local `log.txt`.
- **Perf export** creates a local `perf_export.txt` containing system
  information, your FarmMonitor configuration, and performance measurements.

These files are stored **only on your computer** and are **never uploaded
automatically**. If you choose to share a diagnostic file for a bug report,
you upload it yourself — this is always a manual, voluntary action.

## Third-party assets

The dashboard loads icon fonts from the jsdelivr CDN at runtime. As with any
web request, the CDN provider may receive your IP address. FarmMonitor itself
does not send any data to the CDN beyond the standard asset request.
